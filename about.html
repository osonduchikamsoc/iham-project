About the institute

We train the people who administer housing in Nigeria, and we broker the land and property they go on to manage.

15+ Years in practice
Our Story

Built because the standard was not being kept.

The institute was founded on a simple frustration: too many Nigerians were losing money on land and property because nobody in the chain was accountable for the paperwork. Agents sold, buyers paid, and the title turned out to be someone else's.

So we did two things at once. We built a training arm to teach housing administration properly — the law, the process, the duty of care — and we built an estate arm that would be held to exactly what we teach. The two are deliberately inseparable. When our own graduates can audit our listings, cutting corners stops being an option.

Fifteen years on, we have certified more than 1,750 practitioners and completed over 2,400 transactions across Abuja and Lagos. Neither number matters more than the fact that we have never sold a property whose title did not survive independent verification.

Our Mission

To raise the professional standard of housing administration in Nigeria, and to make property ownership safe for ordinary buyers by being accountable for every document that passes through our hands.

Our Vision

A property market where verified title is the default rather than the exception, and where every estate in the country is run by somebody formally trained to run it.

Our Values

Verify before you sell. Say what is actually true about a property, including the inconvenient parts. Stay reachable after the money has changed hands.
