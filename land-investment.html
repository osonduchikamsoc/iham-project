How It Works

From first call to title in your hand.

Four steps, no surprises in between.

1

Book a free site inspection

Pick a date and time on any listing. We meet you at the estate — bring your surveyor or solicitor.

2

Choose your plot and payment plan

Pay outright or spread it over 12 to 18 months. Your plot is reserved from the first instalment.

3

Allocation and pegging

On completion we peg your exact plot with you present and issue the allocation letter.

4

Documentation

Registered survey and deed of assignment prepared in your name and handed over.
