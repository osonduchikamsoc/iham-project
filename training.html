Executive Blue Prints: Remediating Land Fraud and Racketing in FCT Real Estate Sector

All Association in Real Estate Development are invited to join the Institute of Housing Administration and Management Ltd/gte.

This became necessary in order to shape the nature of housing business in Nigeria.

Selling, Buying and Leasing properties is in no means a small issue, and highly challenging to the extent that it requires formidable and well equip techno-adventures and mind blowing technical know-how often seen as a gateway to equable investment in this area.

We are leaving no stone unturned and also not leaving qualified professionals behind but build on analytic of human energy to be able to develop this area well to suite the present stranding in the industry.

To work with interested parties desirous to have vested interest on housing and lands in both short and long term period.

1

SECTION 1: IHAM Notable Features

IHAM Notable Features include operating under a structured policy framework designed to bring sanity, ethical accountability and technical excellence in the sector.

Dual Corporate Status: The company is registered as a limited corporate entity which its primary motive is public interest; sector regulation when given its full legislation by the act of National Assembly and Professional Educational Instrument within a short period.

Comprehensive Policy Framework: Operates across defined core pillars including educational development, professional certification advocacy and lifting of standards for equitable practice in the industry.

Professional Certification: Standardizes housing practices by issuing professional certification to qualified property managers, developers and housing administrators.

Multidisciplinary Scope: Integrate real estate law, digital property management and Financial governance into unified administrative curriculum.

2

SECTION 2: Remedial Mechanisms against Land Malpractices

IHAM will within its scope correct the wide spread illegalities in the country's real estate environment through specific systemic checks.

Eliminating Forged Papers: By establishing a centralized repository of IHAM - certified practitioners where all documentations of housing verifications strictly pass through vetted, legally bound professionals, leaving no room for freelance racketeers.

Protecting Clients From Fraud (danger): Through its clear public guidelines and professional database, property buyers can confirm the status of any real estate developer or agent via the official IHAM portal, before exchanging funds, completely neutralizing non existent housing scams.

3

SECTION 3: Strategic Guidelines To Follow

1. Vetting and Practitioner Selection: IHAM will put mechanisms in place that will verify property managers and developers who holds active IHAM registration status on the IHAM professional registry.

2. Due Diligence and legal Alignment : Ensure that property papers match centralized housing and land documents in FCT, states and the country as a whole.

3. Utilized the exact administrative formats designed by the Institute. All transactions records, tenancy documents or management handovers must deploy the same standard detailed in the books of the agencies concerned.

4

Financial & Systemic Objectives

Deposits And Transfers Of Funds: All funds as transfers and cash deposits must go through the property managers not through the agents and financial dealings must go through accredited bank accounts or designed facilities tied to the corporate developer.

Restoration of Market Confidence: Foreign and local investors will no longer fear losing hard -earned capital to land grabbers and syndicates. Manipulation of Instruments in the Sectors will become a thing of the past.

Drastic Reduction in Court Litigation: The overwhelming volume of land and housing cases, including landlords and agents, tenants blocking the court processes will reduced. This will provide avenue for air tight title tracking and clear-cut administrative standards will be maintained.

IHAM main objectives is to block all loopholes, follow the trend to check incompetencies in the system. We will built on existing mandates to help focus on areas to rid the system of persistent problems in housing.
