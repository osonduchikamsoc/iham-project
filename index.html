IHAM Strategic Framework

Promoting Professional Standards in Housing Administration and Real Estate.

All Association in Real Estate Development are invited to join the Institute of Housing Administration and Management Ltd/gtc. This became necessary in order to shape the nature of housing business in Nigeria.

Selling, Buying and Leasing properties is in no means a small issue, and highly challenging to the extent that it requires formidable and well equip techno-adventures and mind blowing technical know-how often seen as a gateway to equable investment in this area.

We are leaving no stone unturned and also not leaving qualified professionals behind but build on analytic of human energy to be able to develop this area well to suite the present stranding in the industry[cite: 6]. To work with interested parties desirous to have vested interest on housing and lands in both short and long term period.

The Country is marred with litigation and land grabbing with the intention of forge papers, dupe Clients of their money and change tittle documents.

The IHAM is going to set out guidelines to remedy this with its long term benefits. The Institute will serve as Critical Professional Intervention by enforcing professional standards and training certified housing experts. IHAM will act as a Structural remedy to curb corruption and protect real estate investors.

SECTION 1: IHAM Notable Features include operating under a structured policy framework designed to bring sanity, ethical accountability and technical excellence in the sector.

Dual Corporate Status: The company is registered as a limited corporate entity which its primary motive is public interest; sector regulation when given its full legislation by the act of National Assembly and Professional Educational Instrument within a short period.

1

Comprehensive Policy Framework:

Operates across defined core pillars including educational development, professional certification advocacy and lifting of standards for equitable practice in the industry.

2

Professional Certification:

Standardizes housing practices by issuing professional certification to qualified property managers, developers and housing administrators.

3

Multidisciplinary Scope:

Integrate real estate law, digital property management and Financial governance into unified administrative curriculum.

4

SECTION 2: Remedial Mechanisms against Land Malpractices

IHAM will within its scope correct the wide spread illegalities in the country's real estate environment through specific systemic checks.

5

Eliminating Forged Papers:

By establishing a centralized repository of IHAM - certified practitioners where all documentations of housing verifications strictly pass through vetted, legally bound professionals, leaving no room for freelance racketeers.

6

Protecting Clients From Fraud (danger):

Through its clear public guidelines and professional database, property buyers can confirm the status of any real estate developer or agent via the official IHAM portal, before exchanging funds, completely neutralizing non existent housing scams.

7

SECTION 3: Strategic Guidelines To Follow:

1. Vetting and Practitioner Selection: IHAM will put mechanisms in place that will verify property managers and developers who holds active IHAM registration status on the IHAM professional registry.

2. Due Diligence and legal Alignment : Ensure that property papers match centralized housing and land documents in FCT, states and the country as a whole.

3. Utilized the exact administrative formats designed by the Institute. All transactions records, tenancy documents or management handovers must deploy the same standard detailed in the books of the agencies concerned.

Why Trust Us

The expertise behind every transaction.

Land fraud in Nigeria is not a rumour — it is a routine risk. Here is precisely what we do so it does not become your problem.

Title Verification

We run a full search at the state land registry on every property before it is listed, and we show you the result — not a summary of it.

Legal Documentation

Deeds, assignments and consents are prepared by our in-house legal team and registered in your name, not held "pending".

Flexible Payment

Spread land payments over 12 to 18 months with no interest added. Your allocation is reserved from the first instalment.

After-Sale Management

Facility management, tenant sourcing and rent administration if you would rather not run the property yourself.
